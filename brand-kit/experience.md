**Decision:** Use plain, confident English and short, specific headings. Retain “Start a Conversation” for relevant marketing invitations, not every operational button. Tasks use literal verbs such as “Save changes”; errors state the problem and recovery; AI answers distinguish sources from uncertainty.
**Preserve / adapt:** Match the source’s directness without promising quality, cost, timing or integration outcomes that are not established. Demonstration records and results must be labeled illustrative. Never invent customers or quote endorsements.
**Why:** This extends the observed promotional voice into truthful prototype interactions. Production consent/legal copy must be supplied separately, not copied from the website form.
